ACME Corporation — Business Continuity & Backup Policy
Document ID: POL-2025-004
Effective Date: February 1, 2025
Classification: Internal

1. PURPOSE

This policy ensures ACME can continue critical operations and recover data
after a disruption such as a cyberattack, outage, or natural disaster.

2. BACKUP REQUIREMENTS

2.1 Backup Frequency
Production databases must be backed up every 6 hours. File servers and
collaboration platforms must be backed up daily.

2.2 Backup Retention
Backups are retained for 35 days. Month-end backups of financial systems are
retained for 12 months.

2.3 Offsite Storage
At least one copy of every backup must be stored in a geographically
separate region, at least 200 miles from the primary data center, and must be
immutable for the length of its retention period.

2.4 Backup Encryption
All backups must be encrypted with AES-256. Backup encryption keys must be
stored separately from the backup media.

3. RECOVERY OBJECTIVES

3.1 Tier A Systems
Tier A systems (payments, customer portal, and authentication) must have a
recovery time objective (RTO) of 4 hours and a recovery point objective (RPO)
of 15 minutes.

3.2 Tier B Systems
Tier B systems (HR, internal reporting, and CRM) must have an RTO of 24 hours
and an RPO of 6 hours.

3.3 Tier C Systems
Tier C systems (development and test environments) have an RTO of 5 business
days and no defined RPO.

4. TESTING

4.1 Restore Testing
Restores must be tested quarterly for Tier A systems and annually for Tier B
systems. Test results are reported to the Risk Committee.

4.2 Disaster Recovery Exercises
A full disaster recovery exercise must be run once a year and must include
failover of at least one Tier A system to the secondary region.

5. CONTINUITY PLANNING

5.1 Business Impact Analysis
Each department head must update the department's business impact analysis
every 12 months or after a major organizational change.

5.2 Crisis Communication
During a declared incident, the Crisis Management Team lead is the only
person authorized to issue external statements. Employees are notified
through the emergency notification system and the backup phone tree.

5.3 Pandemic and Facility Loss
If an office is unavailable, employees assigned to critical functions must
switch to remote work within one business day under the Acceptable Use &
Remote Work Policy.

6. LOGGING

6.1 Log Retention
Security and audit logs must be retained online for 90 days and in archive
storage for a total of 13 months.
