ACME Corporation — Privacy & Data Subject Rights Policy
Document ID: POL-2025-005
Effective Date: April 1, 2025
Classification: Internal

1. PURPOSE

This policy describes how ACME handles requests from individuals exercising
their rights over personal data under GDPR, CCPA, and similar laws.

2. RECEIVING REQUESTS

2.1 Request Channels
Requests may be submitted through the privacy web form, by email to
privacy@acme.example.com, or by phone. Any employee who receives a request
must forward it to the Privacy Office within two (2) business days.

2.2 Identity Verification
The Privacy Office must verify the requester's identity before releasing or
deleting any data. At least two identifying data points must match the record
on file.

3. RESPONSE DEADLINES

3.1 Acknowledgement
Every request must be acknowledged in writing within 5 business days.

3.2 Response Time
ACME must complete the request within 30 calendar days of receipt. For
complex requests this may be extended by up to 60 additional days, and the
requester must be told about the extension within the first 30 days.

3.3 Fees
No fee is charged for a request. A reasonable fee may be charged only for
requests that are manifestly unfounded or excessive.

4. REQUEST TYPES

4.1 Access Requests
The individual receives a copy of the personal data ACME holds, the purposes
of processing, and the categories of recipients.

4.2 Deletion Requests
Personal data is deleted from production systems within 30 days of an
approved request. Backup copies are overwritten at the end of the normal
backup cycle. Data that ACME is legally required to keep is not deleted, and
the requester is told why.

4.3 Opt-Out of Sale or Sharing
ACME does not sell personal data. Requests to opt out of sharing for
advertising must be honored within 15 business days.

5. BREACHES AFFECTING PERSONAL DATA

5.1 Regulator Notification
The Privacy Office, with Legal, must notify the relevant supervisory authority
within 72 hours of becoming aware of a personal data breach, unless the
breach is unlikely to result in a risk to individuals.

5.2 Individual Notification
Where a breach is likely to result in a high risk to individuals, they must
be notified without undue delay.

6. CHILDREN'S DATA

6.1 Minimum Age
ACME services are not directed at children under 16. If ACME learns it holds
data about a child under 16 collected without parental consent, that data
must be deleted within 14 days.

7. RECORDS

7.1 Request Log
The Privacy Office keeps a log of all requests and responses for six (6)
years.
