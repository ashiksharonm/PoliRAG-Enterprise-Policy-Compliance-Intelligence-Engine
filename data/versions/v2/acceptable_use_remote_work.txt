ACME Corporation — Acceptable Use & Remote Work Policy
Document ID: POL-2025-002
Effective Date: January 1, 2025
Classification: Internal

1. PURPOSE

This policy defines acceptable use of company devices, networks, and
accounts, and the additional controls that apply when employees work
outside an ACME office.

2. DEVICES

2.1 Company-Issued Laptops
All laptops must use full-disk encryption and must lock automatically after
10 minutes of inactivity. Lost or stolen devices must be reported to the IT
Service Desk within 4 hours so the device can be remotely wiped.

2.2 Personal Devices (BYOD)
Personal smartphones may access company email only after enrolling in the
mobile device management (MDM) program. Personal laptops and tablets are not
permitted to connect to production systems or to store Confidential or
Restricted data.

2.3 Removable Media
USB storage devices are blocked by default. Exceptions require written
approval from Information Security and are limited to 30 days.

3. NETWORK AND REMOTE ACCESS

3.1 VPN Requirement
Employees working remotely must connect through the corporate VPN before
accessing any internal application. Split tunneling is prohibited.

3.2 Public Wi-Fi
Employees may use public Wi-Fi only when the corporate VPN is active.
Company data must never be accessed over a public network without it.

3.3 Remote Work Locations
Employees may work remotely from any country on the approved-locations list
for up to 30 consecutive days per calendar year. Work from a country that is
not on the list requires prior approval from both Human Resources and
Information Security.

4. ACCEPTABLE USE

4.1 Personal Use
Limited personal use of company systems is permitted if it does not
interfere with work, consume significant bandwidth, or violate any other
policy. Streaming of non-work video during working hours is not permitted.

4.2 Prohibited Activities
Employees must not install unlicensed software, attempt to bypass security
controls, mine cryptocurrency, or use company systems to access illegal
content.

4.3 Generative AI Tools
Employees may use only company-approved generative AI tools for work. Source
code, customer data, and any information classified Confidential or
Restricted must not be entered into public AI chatbots.

5. MONITORING

5.1 Monitoring Notice
ACME may monitor network traffic, email, and system activity on company
resources. Employees have no expectation of privacy when using company
systems, except where local law provides otherwise.

6. SEPARATION

6.1 Return of Assets
On separation, all company devices and credentials must be returned or
disabled by the employee's last working day. Accounts are deactivated within
24 hours of the separation date.
