ACME Corporation — Anti-Bribery, Gifts & Conflicts of Interest Policy
Document ID: POL-2025-006
Effective Date: January 15, 2025
Classification: Internal

1. PURPOSE

ACME prohibits bribery and corruption in all forms and requires employees to
avoid conflicts between personal interests and the interests of the company.

2. GIFTS AND HOSPITALITY

2.1 Gifts Received
Employees may accept gifts with a market value of up to $50 from a single
source in a calendar year. Gifts above that value must be declined or handed
to the Compliance department. Cash and cash equivalents, such as gift cards,
may never be accepted.

2.2 Gifts Given
Gifts to customers or business partners must not exceed $100 per recipient
per year and must be recorded in the gift register. Any gift to a government
official requires prior written approval from Legal, regardless of value.

2.3 Meals and Entertainment
Business meals are permitted when reasonable and related to a business
purpose. Entertainment with an estimated value over $250 per person requires
prior approval from the employee's director.

3. GOVERNMENT OFFICIALS

3.1 Facilitation Payments
Facilitation (grease) payments to speed up routine government actions are
prohibited, even where local practice allows them.

3.2 Third-Party Intermediaries
Agents, consultants, and distributors who deal with government officials on
ACME's behalf must complete anti-corruption due diligence and sign an
anti-corruption certification every year.

4. CONFLICTS OF INTEREST

4.1 Disclosure
Employees must disclose any actual or potential conflict of interest to their
manager and to Compliance within 10 business days of becoming aware of it.

4.2 Outside Employment
Outside employment or board service requires written approval from the
employee's manager and Compliance. Work for a competitor, customer, or
supplier of ACME is not permitted.

4.3 Family Relationships
An employee may not directly supervise, or take part in hiring or contract
decisions about, a family member. Such relationships must be disclosed.

4.4 Financial Interests
Holding more than 1% of the shares of a publicly traded supplier or competitor
must be disclosed. Investments in private suppliers or competitors require
approval from Compliance.

5. POLITICAL AND CHARITABLE CONTRIBUTIONS

5.1 Political Contributions
ACME funds may not be used for political contributions without written
approval from the General Counsel.

6. REPORTING AND CONSEQUENCES

6.1 Reporting Concerns
Concerns may be reported to Compliance or to the anonymous compliance
hotline. ACME prohibits retaliation against anyone who reports in good faith.

6.2 Training
All employees complete anti-corruption training within 30 days of hire and
every 12 months after that.
