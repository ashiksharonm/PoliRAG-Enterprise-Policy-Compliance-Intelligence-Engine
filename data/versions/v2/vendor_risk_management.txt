ACME Corporation — Third-Party & Vendor Risk Management Policy
Document ID: POL-2025-003
Effective Date: March 1, 2025
Classification: Internal

1. PURPOSE

This policy sets the due diligence, contracting, and monitoring requirements
for third parties that provide services to ACME or process ACME data.

2. VENDOR TIERING

2.1 Tier Definitions
Tier 1 (Critical) vendors process Confidential or Restricted data or support
a business-critical system. Tier 2 (Important) vendors process Internal data
only. Tier 3 (Low) vendors have no access to ACME data or systems.

2.2 Tier Assignment
The business owner proposes a tier and Procurement confirms it. Information
Security may raise a vendor's tier but may not lower it.

3. DUE DILIGENCE

3.1 Security Assessment
Tier 1 vendors must complete the full security questionnaire and provide a
current SOC 2 Type II report or ISO 27001 certificate before contract
signature. Tier 2 vendors complete the short-form questionnaire.

3.3 Reassessment Cycle
Tier 1 vendors are reassessed every 12 months. Tier 2 vendors are
reassessed every 24 months. Tier 3 vendors are not reassessed.

4. CONTRACT REQUIREMENTS

4.1 Mandatory Clauses
Every contract with a vendor that handles personal data must include a data
processing agreement, a right-to-audit clause, and a requirement to notify
ACME of a security incident within 48 hours of discovery.

4.2 Subcontractors
A vendor may not engage a subcontractor to process ACME data without prior
written approval from ACME. Approved subcontractors are bound by the same
obligations as the vendor.

4.3 Contract Approval Thresholds
Contracts up to $50,000 per year may be approved by a department head.
Contracts above $50,000 and up to $250,000 per year require approval from
the VP of Procurement. Contracts above $250,000 per year require approval
from the Chief Financial Officer.

5. OFFBOARDING

5.1 Data Return and Destruction
When a vendor relationship ends, the vendor must return or destroy all ACME
data within 30 days and provide a written certificate of destruction.

5.2 Access Removal
Information Security must revoke all vendor accounts and network access on
the termination date of the contract.

6. RECORDS

6.1 Vendor File Retention
Vendor contracts, assessments, and certificates of destruction must be
retained for seven (7) years after the contract ends.
